01 — THE PROBLEM

Physical AI has no release process.

Software solved this twenty years ago. Write a test, gate the merge, ship on green. The evidence is produced by the pipeline, it is the same every time, and nobody has to be in the room for it to count.

Autonomous systems still ship on judgement. A demo that went well. A field trial that did not crash. A simulation job that someone re-ran until it passed. Each of those is an impression of safety, and none of them survives being asked to prove it — to a customer, to an insurer, or to yourself at three in the morning after an incident.

That does not scale with fleet size, and it does not transfer between teams. It is the bottleneck between a robot that works and a robot you can ship.

02 — WHAT WE DO

We make simulation produce evidence, not impressions.

01

Deterministic verdicts

Same seed, same architecture, same answer. A result you cannot reproduce is not evidence — it is an anecdote with a chart attached.

02

Reason codes, not red

A failure names what moved against a recorded baseline. “The build is broken” is not a finding. “Minimum clearance fell below baseline” is.

03

Evidence that travels

Verdicts, reason codes and the runs behind them are artifacts you can hand to someone else. Designed to support the safety case you have to make — never a claim to have made it for you.

04 — HOW WE WORK

We look like a test organisation, not a software brand.

What we sell is confidence in measurements, so our own artifacts have to behave like records. These are not aspirations — they are the rules our documents are checked against before they leave the building.

01

Numbers over adjectives

If a claim has no unit, we cut it or we measure it. “High fidelity” is not a specification.

02

Corrections stay visible

When a published number changes, the old value stays — struck through, with the revision named beside it. Revising in the open is the credibility.

03

Every sheet is signed

Preparer, date, revision. An anonymous document is an unfinished document.

04

Designed to support, never certified

We build evidence for the safety case you have to make. We do not claim to have made it for you, and we will say so in writing.

05

We publish what is not in hand

Every partner gets the list of what we have not built and cannot yet measure, next to the list of what we have. Finding out late is the expensive way.
