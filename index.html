01
01 — THE PROBLEM

How do you know the new build is safe?

Today the answer is 30 to 100 real-robot trials — expensive, slow, and never quite the same twice. Or it is a simulation job in CI that goes red often enough that someone re-runs it until it is green.

Neither is evidence. Both are how a regression reaches a live floor.

02
02 — METHOD · ALL FOUR STEPS SHIP TODAY

Four steps, one exit code.

STEP 01

Bring your platform

Your URDF and the behaviours that actually matter. We assemble the scenes.

STEP 02

Encode behaviours

Each becomes a scenario spec with an explicit pass/fail condition, living in your repo.

STEP 03

Gate the merge

A GitHub Action runs the scenarios on every pull request. The verdict is the exit code.

STEP 04

Read the reason

A failed verdict comes back reason-coded against a recorded baseline — not just red.
